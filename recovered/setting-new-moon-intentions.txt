Setting new moon intentions
Oct 18, 2022
==================================================

Each new moon is a time for setting new intention(s) for the 4 weeks ahead.

This can be how you want to feel, something you want to do, a job you really want to get… anything that feels right for you.

What helps me is looking at my big goals for the year and thinking about small things that I can achieve that help me towards my big goals.

Write these intentions as though they’re already happening. Say them aloud.

“I always speak my truth no matter what”
“I attract abundance in all areas of my life”
“I savour every single second”
“I say yes to more things that excite me”

Accept whatever comes to your mind when thinking about this as the truth, without doubts. This new moon is ESPECIALLY about being in the flow of life. Don’t waste your bursting summer energy pushing or forcing anything that doesn’t feel right.

Let your summer energy and this next moon cycle make your wildest dreams a reality!
