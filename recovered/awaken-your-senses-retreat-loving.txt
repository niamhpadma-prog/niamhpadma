Life is feeling flowier than a waterfall. Could you say a waterfall is flowy? What’s flowier than a waterfall? Not sure but you get what I’m trying to say.

There are moments in my days where I feel like I am observing myself flowing through life. Moments that should be difficult & cause stress I just smile & bless the situation with love. Each section of life fitting perfectly together like a jigsaw piece.

Sometimes I feel like my direction is so powerful that it just does the driving for me & I can just sit back & take in the view.

The course of events that has happened since I started walking in the direction of what I clearly valued rather than what other people valued has resulted in an immense shift from feeling like I was pushing a door that was meant to be pulled.

I work hard. But it doesn’t feel hard. I love (almost) everything I do & if something is feeling off I know I have the power to change that. At the same time knowing that everything in life ultimately is there to show / teach us something. Most of the time still trying to figure out what that thing is 🙃

Align your values. Follow a path that feels true to you. Dedicate yourself to that path. Shed the baggage that is holding you back from it. Step into the car & enjoy the ride. Life has so many beautiful things in store if you let it show you the way.

Photos from ‘Awaken Your Senses / Despierto Tus Sentidos’ retreat. Which was really just a birthing from a conversation one afternoon with the intention of bringing people together in connection, then catching the wave and riding it. Somehow we ended up sharing the most intimate beautiful moments with 10 other uniquely gorgeous souls on the beach of Argentina (with a lot of planning, a whole ton of preparation, a little healthy stress, & SO MUCH love in between)

Hasta la próxima amores. I don’t think this feeling of bliss is going to end any time soon.

If you’ve read this far I love you 🥰
